Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

1. Data we process

The App connects to your TikTok account only through TikTok's official Content Posting API with your explicit authorization (OAuth). The App receives and processes:

  • Your TikTok user identifier (open_id) and basic public profile information,
  • Video files and captions that you or the automation pipeline prepare for publishing.

2. Data storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator's own device, in an encrypted environment file. Video files are stored locally and uploaded to TikTok only when you trigger publishing. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

3. Data retention

Tokens are refreshed automatically and can be revoked at any time from your TikTok account settings. Local copies of videos are kept only as long as needed for the publishing pipeline.

4. Your rights

You may revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok account settings. Contact us to request deletion of any stored data.
